# Full Analysis and Conditional Presentation Support

Use for a coherent breakdown of one paper, or for presentation aids explicitly requested from its analysis. This mode does not require the user to answer checks section by section. Follow the entrypoint's mode/scope precedence and source, language, and write boundaries.

## Read to Explain the Argument

Start with the paper's identity, version, and accessible scope. Use navigation tools to find the central argument, then read substantive model/method and results or theoretical evidence. An outline or abstract is not the whole paper. Use the [summary schema](summary-template.md) to organize the output; adapt it rather than filling every field.

Explain the problem through an accessible scenario, difficulty, and central idea, then connect the needed background, claimed contributions, assumptions, method, evidence, and limits. Explain why important steps or modules exist. For equations, prioritize meaning, purpose, and applicability conditions; expand symbols or derivations where needed. Theoretical and survey papers need their actual evidence, not invented experiments or a forced training/loss section.

Inspect original pages for key equations, figures, or tables used in conclusions. Record locations and distinguish what was read, visually checked, or left unreviewed. Read evidence alongside its metrics, comparators, and conditions; keep author claims, checked support, and assistant interpretation distinct. Novelty claims need not be independently established. Consult external literature when a requested judgment requires it, not as an automatic unbounded review.

Use selective small numerical examples or concise diagrams when they clarify a central relationship. Label invented values and teaching simplifications, source-locate relationships, and distinguish task, physical, and auxiliary graphs where relevant. Respect pure text and requested length; do not force an example for every equation or a diagram for every section.

## Completion and Partial Sources

Call the requested analysis complete only when its main problem, contribution, method, evidence, and limits are covered with source locations and the reviewed scope is disclosed. This practical gate does not require exhaustive proof or supplement auditing. For a lengthy source, prioritize the central argument and requested deliverable, and identify material not reviewed.

With only an abstract/excerpt, missing pages, unreadable plots, or other evidence gaps, provide a clearly labeled partial analysis of the supported parts and identify what is needed to complete it. Do not manufacture a method, experimental number, or guarantee. An available full PDF does not establish that it has all been read. A deadline does not remove these boundaries. Do not claim proof verification, reproduction, comprehensive novelty review, or user mastery without the corresponding work.

On same-paper mode changes, reuse verified context and inspect missing evidence. Preserve reading position without mistaking earlier partial coverage for full analysis. After completed analysis, a request for an outline or Q&A is a follow-up deliverable, not a reason to restart every section.

## Presentation Output Only When Requested

Plain full analysis produces the paper analysis and summary only: no outline, slide plan, anticipated Q&A, speaking script, or assumed talk length. Natural-language intent can request presentation support without exact keywords; follow the requested component and exclusions.

| Prompt or context | Output scope |
| --- | --- |
| "拆论文", "完整分析模式" alone | Analysis and summary, no presentation aids or duration assumption |
| "汇报提纲", "逐页提纲", or a clear request for a talk outline | Outline with relevant paper content; no automatic Q&A |
| "可能被问什么", "预期问答", "模拟老师提问" | Potential questions and source-grounded answers; no automatic slide outline |
| "明天汇报，准备汇报材料" or equivalent explicit package request | May include analysis, outline, and Q&A as a presentation package; state organizational assumptions briefly |
| Analysis plus specified aids, or "只分析，不要提纲/问答" | Add only the requested aids; honor exclusions |
| "总结一下", "整理一下" | Scoped chat recap; no mode escalation or implied save |

Only for requested presentation support, use the stated audience, duration, focus, and length. If no duration is given, assume roughly a 10-minute talk for organizing that support and mention the assumption without blocking. This is a talk-length assumption, never a processing-time promise. Make no duration assumption for plain analysis.

For a requested outline, give each slide/topic a takeaway and speaking points, and recommend actual source figures/tables with location and purpose when useful. Do not invent figures or imply the deck already exists. For requested Q&A, distinguish authors' claims, checked evidence, inference, and unknowns; do not fabricate the user's opinions or experimental work. The independently optional sections in the summary schema provide output structure.

Chat is the default. A presentation request does not itself authorize creating a PPT, notes, or other file. Export only when explicitly requested to a specified destination, using appropriate available tools and preserving no-overwrite and Obsidian boundaries.
