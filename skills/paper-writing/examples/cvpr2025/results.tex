\section{Experimental Results}
\label{sec:results}

Dean and Barroso describe how latency variability can affect the
responsiveness of interactive services~\cite{dean2013tail}.
To show the variation behind each mean, we plot the individual observations
in Fig.~\ref{fig:latency}. The observations are synthetic per-image latency
records for three illustrative preprocessing groups.

Table~\ref{tab:latency} summarizes the fixture. The sequential and batched
means were \SequentialMean{} and \BatchedMean{} ms, respectively, a
sequential-minus-batched difference of \SequentialMinusBatched{} ms.
The reference group averaged \ReferenceMean{} ms.
These comparisons describe the supplied samples.
